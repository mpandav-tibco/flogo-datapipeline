00

Strategy & Information Architecture

The frame that every screen serves. Two jobs, one model: engineers author & test pipelines; operators run, watch & recover them — on the same canvas, with the data always visible.

Who we design for

Priya — Integration / Data Engineer primary
Builds a Source → Transform → Sink pipeline, needs to see records flow and trust the output before shipping.
Marco — Platform / DataOps primary
Runs a fleet of pipelines; needs health, throughput, incremental position, and fast failure triage.
Dana — Data Steward secondary
Cares about quality & rejects — DQ scorecards, the DLQ, and why rows were dropped.

Design principles (best-in-class bar)

1
Glass-box, not black-box. Show the data at every stage — live samples, before/after — not just row counts.
2
Design ↔ Run parity. The authoring canvas is the observability canvas; nodes light up with live throughput.
3
The funnel always reconciles. processed = written + dropped + routed + failed, on every run. No silent loss.
4
Progressive disclosure. One inspector panel; depth on demand. No modal mazes, no raw-JSON gymnastics.
5
Guardrails before commit. Dry-run preview, validate, DQ gate, and a DLQ so bad rows are captured — never lost.
6
Operate at fleet scale. Status, throughput & watermark across many pipelines; keyboard-first, real-time.

Connections ≠ pipelines. Credentials are defined once, reused everywhere, and show usage count so nothing breaks silently when rotated. Secrets are never rendered.
